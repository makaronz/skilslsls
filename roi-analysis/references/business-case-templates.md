# Business Case Templates

Structured templates for building compelling business cases that justify investments and initiatives.

---

## Standard Business Case Template

### Executive Summary

Write this last but present it first. Summarize in one paragraph:
- What you propose
- Why it matters (problem and opportunity)
- Expected return (headline ROI or NPV)
- Required investment
- Recommended decision

### Problem Statement

| Element | Content |
|---------|---------|
| Current State | Describe the current situation with specific metrics |
| Pain Points | List 3-5 specific problems with quantified impact |
| Root Cause | Explain why the current state exists |
| Cost of Inaction | What happens if we do nothing? Quantify the ongoing cost |

### Proposed Solution

| Element | Content |
|---------|---------|
| Description | Clear explanation of the proposed solution |
| Scope | What is included and explicitly excluded |
| Approach | High-level implementation plan |
| Timeline | Phase-by-phase timeline with milestones |
| Dependencies | What must be true for this to succeed |

### Risk Assessment

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Implementation delay | Medium | Medium | Agile approach with phased delivery |
| Adoption resistance | High | High | Change management program, executive sponsorship |
| Cost overrun | Medium | Medium | Fixed-price vendor contract, 15% contingency |
| Technology failure | Low | High | Proof of concept, fallback plan |

### Recommendation

Clearly state the recommended decision with supporting rationale and the ask (budget, headcount, timeline approval).

### Benefits Quantification

Categorize benefits by type and confidence:

| Benefit | Type | Annual Value | Confidence | Basis |
|---------|------|-------------|-----------|-------|
| FTE time savings | Hard (measurable) | $200K | High | Process analysis |
| Faster time-to-market | Hard (measurable) | $500K | Medium | Industry benchmark |
| Improved decision quality | Soft (estimated) | $300K | Low | Management estimate |
| Risk reduction | Strategic | $150K | Medium | Incident cost history |

Hard benefits have a clear measurement method. Soft benefits require assumptions. Strategic benefits are real but difficult to quantify directly.

## Quick Business Case (One-Page)

For smaller investments (<$50K), use a one-page format:

| Section | Content | Length |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Problem | What problem are we solving? | 2-3 sentences |
| Solution | What do we propose? | 2-3 sentences |
| Investment | What does it cost? | Single number + breakdown |
| Return | What do we get back? | ROI %, payback period |
| Risk | What could go wrong? | Top 2-3 risks |
| Ask | What decision is needed? | Approve/reject with budget amount |
