### Creative Best Practices

#### Visual Design

- **Mobile-first**: Design for small screens — use large text, clear imagery
- **Brand consistency**: Maintain brand colors, fonts, and tone across creatives
- **Text overlay**: Keep text minimal — under 20% of image area
- **Contrast**: Ensure CTAs stand out against backgrounds
- **Authenticity**: Use real imagery over stock photos when possible

#### Video Creative

- **Hook in 3 seconds**: Capture attention immediately
- **Sound-off design**: Use captions and visual storytelling
- **Vertical format**: Prioritize 9:16 for mobile feeds and stories
- **Clear CTA**: End with a strong call-to-action
- **Branding early**: Show brand within first 3 seconds

#### Copy Guidelines

- **Headlines**: 5–8 words, benefit-driven
- **Primary text**: Front-load the value proposition
- **CTA buttons**: Match CTA to campaign objective
- **A/B testing**: Test headlines, descriptions, and CTAs independently

| Pitfall | Why It Happens | How to Avoid |
