# Perspective Correction

Fix converging verticals and distortion in architectural photography.

---

## In-Camera (Tilt-Shift)
1. Level camera perfectly
2. Frame with building top cut off
3. Shift lens upward to include top
4. Verticals stay parallel (camera is level)

## Lightroom Transform

| Tool | Use | Precision |
|------|-----|-----------|
| Auto | Quick mild correction | Moderate |
| Vertical | Fix converging verticals | Good |
| Full | Both horizontal and vertical | Good |
| Guided | Draw reference lines | Excellent |

### Guided Upright
1. Click Guided tool in Transform panel
2. Draw 2 vertical lines on building edges
3. Optionally draw 2 horizontal lines
4. Image warps to make lines parallel

## Photoshop Methods
- Edit > Perspective Warp: Draw grid, adjust handles
- Free Transform > Perspective: Drag corners
- Adaptive Wide Angle: For panorama correction

## Quality Notes
- Every correction crops the image
- Shoot 10-20% wider than final frame
- Higher megapixel provides more crop room
- Apply sharpening after correction
- Tilt-shift in-camera has zero quality loss
