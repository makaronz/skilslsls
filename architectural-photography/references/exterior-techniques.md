# Architectural Exterior Techniques

Capture building exteriors with technical precision and artistic vision.

---

## Timing and Light

| Time | Quality | Best For |
|------|---------|----------|
| Blue hour (pre-dawn) | Cool, dramatic | Buildings with interior lighting |
| Golden hour | Warm, directional | East/west facades |
| Overcast | Soft, even | Detail shots |
| Midday | Harsh overhead | Avoid for most shots |
| Twilight | Ambient + artificial balance | Most dramatic results |

### Twilight Technique
1. Arrive 30 minutes before sunset
2. Establish composition with test shots
3. Shoot when interior lights match sky brightness
4. Optimal window: 15-25 minutes
5. Bracket exposures (+/- 2 stops) and blend

---

## Composition

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|------------|
| Symmetry | Center-align symmetrical buildings |
| Leading lines | Roads, paths to building |
| Context | Include environment for scale |
| Framing | Archways, trees as frames |
| Negative space | Sky area for breathing room |
| Human scale | People for size reference |

## Lens Selection

| Lens | Use | Distortion |
|------|-----|-----------|
| Tilt-shift 17-24mm | Primary architectural | Correct in-camera |
| Ultra-wide 14-16mm | Dramatic perspective | Correct in post |
| 24-35mm | Natural perspective | Minor corrections |
| 70-200mm | Detail isolation | Minimal |

## Technical Settings
- Aperture: f/8-f/11 (sharpest range)
- ISO: 100 (minimum noise)
- Focus: Manual at hyperfocal distance
- Format: RAW 14-bit
- Bracketing: 3-5 exposures, 2 stops apart
