# Interior Architecture Photography

Capture interiors with proper exposure, perspective, and lighting balance.

---

## Mixed Lighting

| Source | Color Temp | Solution |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| Windows | 5500K | Base exposure |
| Tungsten | 2700-3200K | Gel flash or blend |
| Fluorescent | 4000-5000K | Replace or gel |
| LED | 2700-6500K | Check individually |

## Exposure Methods

### Ambient-Only
- Tripod, long exposures, bracket for windows vs interior
- Blend exposures for dynamic range
- Best for residential, editorial

### Flash + Ambient (Flambient)
1. Expose for ambient (windows correct)
2. Flash to fill shadows
3. Bounce off ceiling/walls for soft fill
4. Blend layers in Photoshop

### Window Pull
1. Expose for interior (windows blown)
2. Second exposure for windows (interior dark)
3. Mask window exposure into interior

---

## Composition Rules
- Keep vertical lines perfectly vertical (tilt-shift or post-correction)
- Camera at chest height for natural perspective
- Include doorways/hallways for depth
- One-point or two-point perspective

## Room Preparation
- Remove personal clutter
- Clean all surfaces
- Turn on designed lighting
- Replace burned-out bulbs
- Consistent doors open/closed
- Straighten all furniture
- Add fresh flowers/plants if appropriate
