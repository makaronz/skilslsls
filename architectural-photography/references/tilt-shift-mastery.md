# Tilt-Shift Lens Mastery

Expert techniques for tilt-shift lenses in architectural photography.

---

## Shift Function
Moves lens image circle relative to sensor without angling camera.

| Direction | Application |
|-----------|------------|
| Up | Include building tops, camera stays level |
| Down | Include foreground from elevation |
| Left/Right | Avoid camera reflection in glass |
| Left/Right | Create stitched panoramas |

Maximum shift: typically 11-12mm. Stay within 10mm for best edge quality.

## Tilt Function
Angles focal plane for depth of field control.

| Application | Tilt Direction | Effect |
|-------------|---------------|--------|
| Max DOF | Tilt forward (Scheimpflug) | Floor-to-ceiling sharp at wider aperture |
| Miniature | Tilt backward | Very narrow DOF band |
| Product plane | Match product surface | Entire flat subject sharp at f/4 |

## Shift Stitching (Panorama)
1. Camera level on tripod
2. Shoot shifted full left
3. Center
4. Full right
5. Stitch 3 frames = ~2.5x resolution
6. No perspective distortion

## Workflow
1. Mount on sturdy tripod
2. Level camera precisely
3. Compose at zero shift/tilt
4. Apply shift for perspective
5. Apply tilt for DOF if needed
6. Focus manually at critical point
7. f/8-f/11 for sharpness
8. Cable release to eliminate shake
