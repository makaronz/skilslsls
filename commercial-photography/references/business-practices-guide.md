# Commercial Photography Business Practices

Professional operations for commercial photography business.

---

## Pricing Models

| Model | Description | Best For |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| Day rate | Fixed fee per day | Editorial, corporate |
| Per image | Fee per final image | E-commerce, product |
| Project fee | Flat fee | Campaigns |
| Licensing | Usage-based | Advertising |

## Usage Licensing

| Usage | Multiplier | Scope |
|-------|-----------|-------|
| Social only | 1x | Client digital channels |
| Web + social | 1.5x | All digital |
| Print limited | 2x | Specific publications |
| Print unlimited | 3-4x | Any print |
| Full buyout | 5-10x | All usage, perpetual |

---

## Project Workflow

| Phase | Activities | Deliverable |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| Inquiry | Respond in 24h, qualify | Availability |
| Proposal | Scope, pricing, terms | Written estimate |
| Booking | Contract, 50% deposit | Signed contract |
| Pre-production | Shot list, scout, talent | Call sheet |
| Production | Shoot day(s) | On-set selects |
| Post-production | Edit, retouch, deliver | Final images |
| Closing | Final invoice, archive | Thank you |

## Contract Essentials
- Scope of work and deliverables
- Timeline and deadlines
- Pricing and payment terms
- Usage rights and licensing
- Cancellation and revision policy
- Model releases responsibility
- Liability and insurance

## Professional Standards
- Always use written contracts
- Carry liability insurance ($1-2M)
- Back up to 3 locations (3-2-1 rule)
- Deliver on time, communicate delays early
- Request testimonials from satisfied clients
