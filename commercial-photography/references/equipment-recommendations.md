# Commercial Photography Equipment

Essential gear for professional commercial photography.

---

## Camera Bodies

| Feature | Need | Spec |
|---------|------|------|
| Resolution | Large prints, cropping | 45MP+ ideal |
| Dynamic range | Recover highlights/shadows | 14+ stops |
| Tethering | Client review | USB-C support |
| Dual slots | Reliability | CFexpress + SD |

## Lens Kit

| Lens | Aperture | Use |
|------|----------|-----|
| 24-70mm | f/2.8 | General purpose |
| 70-200mm | f/2.8 | Portraits, compression |
| 16-35mm | f/2.8 | Architecture, interiors |
| 90-105mm macro | f/2.8 | Product detail, food |
| Tilt-shift 24mm | f/3.5 | Architecture, product |

## Lighting

| Type | Power | Use | Budget |
|------|-------|-----|--------|
| Speedlight | 60-200Ws | Location fill | $200-600 |
| Compact mono | 300-500Ws | Studio products | $400-1200 |
| Pro monolight | 500-1000Ws | All commercial | $1000-3000 |
| Battery strobe | 400-600Ws | Location, no power | $1000-2500 |

## Studio Essentials
- Seamless paper (white, gray, black) 9ft wide
- Shooting table (white acrylic) for small products
- V-flats (white/black foam core) for bounce/flag
- C-stands, heavy light stands, boom arm
- Tethering setup: cable, lock, laptop with Capture One
