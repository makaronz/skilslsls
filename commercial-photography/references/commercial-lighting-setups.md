# Commercial Lighting Setups

Professional lighting for advertising, catalog, and corporate photography.

---

## Advertising Hero Setup

| Light | Position | Modifier | Purpose |
|-------|----------|----------|---------|
| Key | 45 degrees camera-left | 48 inch softbox | Main illumination |
| Fill | Camera-right | Silver reflector | Shadow control |
| Rim | Behind product | Strip box with grid | Edge definition |
| Background | Behind backdrop | Gelled or bare | Mood and color |
| Accent | Above or side | Snoot or fresnel | Feature highlight |

## Corporate Headshot

| Light | Position | Modifier |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Key | 30-45 degrees | Medium softbox or beauty dish |
| Fill | Camera-right | Silver reflector |
| Hair/rim | Behind subject | Strip box or snoot |
| Background | At backdrop | Even wash |

## Food Photography
- Side/backlight from 4-5 o'clock for texture
- Large diffusion panel simulates window light
- Dark and moody: minimal fill, dark background
- Flash duration 1/2000s+ for pouring liquid shots

## Equipment Notes
- Match all sources to same color temp (5500K)
- Use CTB/CTO gels to match mixed sources
- Shoot gray card for custom white balance
- Monitor with live view histogram

---

## Flash Duration Guide

| Subject | Duration Needed | Equipment |
|---------|----------------|-----------|
| Static product | Any | Any strobe |
| Pouring liquid | 1/2000s+ | Mid-range mono |
| Splash/freeze | 1/5000s+ | High-end mono or speedlight |
| Shattering | 1/8000s+ | Speedlight at low power |
