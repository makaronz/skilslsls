## Sharpness Techniques

### Handholding Long Telephoto

| Technique | Impact | Notes |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| Minimum shutter speed: 1/focal length × 2 | Critical | 600mm → 1/1200s minimum |
| IBIS + OIS combined | +3-5 stops | Enable both when available |
| Proper stance | Significant | Elbows tucked, weight forward |
| Burst mode | Helpful | Middle frames of burst often sharpest |
| Electronic shutter | Slight | Eliminates shutter shock vibration |

### Tripod and Support

| Support | Stability | Mobility | Best For |
|---------|----------|----------|----------|
| Sturdy tripod + gimbal head | Excellent | Low | Blinds, hides, bird photography |
| Monopod | Good | High | Walking safari, birding trails |
| Bean bag | Good | Medium | Vehicle safari, window mount |
| Shoulder stock | Moderate | High | Walking, quick shots |

## Autofocus Strategy

### AF Mode Selection

| Scenario | AF Mode | AF Area | Notes |
|----------|---------|---------|-------|
| Perched bird | Single (AF-S) | Small spot | Precise, on eye |
| Bird in flight | Continuous (AF-C) | Wide/tracking | Let camera track |
| Running mammal | Continuous (AF-C) | Zone/group | Predict path |
| Animal at rest | Single | Spot or eye-detect | Maximum precision |
| Dense foliage | Single, small spot | Single point | Avoid background lock |

### Animal Eye Detection
- Enable animal eye AF if available (Sony, Canon, Nikon mirrorless)
- Works best with clear view of head
- May struggle with small birds or distant subjects
- Fall back to spot AF when eye detect fails

## Exposure for Wildlife

### Quick Settings Reference

| Scenario | Shutter Speed | Aperture | ISO Strategy |
|----------|-------------|----------|-------------|
| Perched bird, good light | 1/500-1/1000 | f/5.6-f/8 | Auto, max 3200 |
| Bird in flight | 1/2000-1/4000 | f/5.6-f/8 | Auto, max 6400 |
| Running mammal | 1/1000-1/2000 | f/4-f/5.6 | Auto, max 6400 |
| Dawn/dusk | 1/250-1/500 | Wide open | Auto, max 12800 |
| Panning motion blur | 1/30-1/125 | f/8-f/16 | Low, auto | 

### Exposure Compensation
- White bird in sun: +1 to +1.5 EV
- Dark bird in sun: -0.5 to -1 EV
- Snow background: +1 to +2 EV
- Dark forest: -0.5 EV
- Backlit subject: +1 to +2 EV (or spot meter on subject)

## Composition at Distance

- Fill the frame: Get as close as ethically possible
- Eye-level perspective: Get low for ground animals
- Catch light in eye: Position so light reflects in subject's eye
- Leave space in direction of gaze/movement
- Clean background: Use wide aperture and distant background
- Include habitat context when appropriate
