# KPI Library by Function

Comprehensive catalog of Key Performance Indicators organized by business function for BI dashboard development.

---

## Finance KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target Benchmark | Frequency |
|-----|---------|-----------------|-----------|
| Gross Margin | (Revenue − COGS) / Revenue × 100 | 50-70% (SaaS), 30-50% (product) | Monthly |
| Operating Margin | Operating Income / Revenue × 100 | 15-25% (healthy business) | Monthly |
| Net Burn Rate | Cash Spent − Cash Earned per month | < 1/12 of runway | Monthly |
| Cash Runway | Cash Balance / Net Burn Rate | > 18 months (startup) | Monthly |
| DSO (Days Sales Outstanding) | (Accounts Receivable / Revenue) × Days | < 45 days | Monthly |
| Quick Ratio (SaaS) | (New MRR + Expansion MRR) / (Churned MRR + Contraction MRR) | > 4 (excellent) | Monthly |
| Rule of 40 | Revenue Growth % + Profit Margin % | > 40% | Quarterly |

## Sales KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target Benchmark | Frequency |
|-----|---------|-----------------|-----------|
| Win Rate | Closed Won / (Closed Won + Closed Lost) × 100 | 20-30% (enterprise) | Monthly |
| Average Deal Size | Total Bookings / Number of Deals | Depends on segment | Monthly |
| Sales Cycle Length | Average days from opportunity creation to close | 30-90 days (mid-market) | Monthly |
| Pipeline Coverage | Pipeline Value / Quota | 3-4× coverage | Weekly |
| Quota Attainment | Actual Bookings / Quota × 100 | 70-80% of reps at 100%+ | Monthly |
| CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) | Total Sales & Marketing Cost / New Customers | < 1/3 of CLV | Quarterly |
| Magic Number | Net New ARR / Prior Quarter S&M Spend | > 0.75 (efficient) | Quarterly |

## Marketing KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target Benchmark | Frequency |
|-----|---------|-----------------|-----------|
| MQLs Generated | Count of Marketing Qualified Leads | Varies by funnel model | Weekly |
| MQL to SQL Conversion | SQLs / MQLs × 100 | 20-30% | Monthly |
| Cost Per Lead (CPL) | Marketing Spend / Leads Generated | Varies by channel | Monthly |
| Marketing Sourced Pipeline | Pipeline $ attributed to marketing | 40-60% of total pipeline | Monthly |
| Website Conversion Rate | Conversions / Visitors × 100 | 2-5% (B2B) | Weekly |
| Email Open Rate | Emails Opened / Emails Delivered × 100 | 20-25% (B2B) | Per campaign |
| Content Engagement | Time on page, scroll depth, shares | Benchmark per content type | Weekly |

## Product KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target Benchmark | Frequency |
|-----|---------|-----------------|-----------|
| DAU/MAU Ratio | Daily Active Users / Monthly Active Users | > 20% (healthy engagement) | Daily |
| Feature Adoption Rate | Users using feature / Total active users × 100 | > 30% for core features | Weekly |
| Time to Value | Days from signup to first value event | < 1 day (PLG) | Weekly |
| NPS (Net Promoter Score) | % Promoters − % Detractors | > 50 (excellent) | Quarterly |
| Session Duration | Average time per session | Depends on app type | Daily |
| Retention Rate (Day 7) | Users active on Day 7 / Users who signed up × 100 | > 20% (mobile), > 40% (SaaS) | Weekly |

## Customer Success KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target Benchmark | Frequency |
|-----|---------|-----------------|-----------|
| Net Revenue Retention (NRR) | (Beginning MRR + Expansion − Churn − Contraction) / Beginning MRR × 100 | > 110% (excellent) | Monthly |
| Gross Revenue Retention (GRR) | (Beginning MRR − Churn − Contraction) / Beginning MRR × 100 | > 90% | Monthly |
| Customer Health Score | Weighted composite of usage, engagement, support | Green/Yellow/Red | Weekly |
| Time to Resolution | Average hours to resolve support tickets | < 4 hours (priority), < 24 hours (standard) | Daily |
| CSAT Score | Satisfied responses / Total responses × 100 | > 85% | Per interaction |

## Using the KPI Library

1. Select 5-8 KPIs per dashboard view — avoid overwhelming users with metrics
2. Pair each KPI with a target, trend, and comparison (prior period or plan)
3. Group related KPIs visually on the dashboard (e.g., all sales KPIs in one section)
4. Define clear ownership: every KPI should have a named accountable person or team
5. Review and retire KPIs quarterly — remove metrics nobody acts on
