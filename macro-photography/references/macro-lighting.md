# Macro Lighting Techniques

Specialized lighting for extreme close-up photography.

---

## Why Macro Lighting Is Different
- Very short working distance (centimeters)
- Standard modifiers too large
- Lens/camera can shadow subject
- Small apertures (f/16-22) reduce light
- Subjects may require even, shadowless illumination

## Solutions

### Ring Flash/Light
- Circular around lens front
- Very even, minimal shadows
- Best for documentation, dental, product detail
- Drawback: flat lighting, ring catchlight

### Twin Flash (Recommended)
- Two heads on either side of lens
- Independent power ratio per head
- Best for insects, flowers, textures
- Setup: 10 and 2 o'clock, 2:1 or 3:1 ratio

### Continuous LED
- See light effect before shooting
- Lower power than flash
- Useful for focus stacking (consistent exposure)

### Diffusion Methods
| Method | Result |
|--------|--------|
| DIY diffuser cup | Softer, more even |
| Mini softbox | Very soft |
| Tissue paper wrap | Budget diffusion |
| Bounce card | Indirect, natural |

## By Subject

### Insects
- Flash at 1/200+ sync to freeze movement
- Twin flash at 2:1 for texture
- f/11-f/16, ISO 200-400

### Flowers
- Natural overcast + reflector
- Backlight for petal glow
- Dark BG: flash on subject only

### Products/Textures
- Ring light for even illumination
- Side light at low angle for texture emphasis
