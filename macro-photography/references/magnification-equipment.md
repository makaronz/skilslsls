# Macro Magnification Equipment

Tools for achieving high magnification in macro photography.

---

## Method Comparison

| Method | Magnification | Quality | Cost |
|--------|-------------|---------|------|
| True macro lens | 1:1-5:1 | Excellent | $$-$$$ |
| Extension tubes | Up to 1:1+ | Good-Excellent | $ |
| Close-up diopters | 0.5:1-1:1 | Moderate-Good | $ |
| Reversing ring | 1:1-3:1+ | Good | $ |
| Bellows | 1:1-5:1+ | Excellent | $$ |

## True Macro Lenses

| Focal Length | Working Distance | Best For |
|-------------|-----------------|---------|
| 40-60mm | 6-10cm | Products, flat subjects |
| 90-105mm | 15-30cm | General macro, insects |
| 150-180mm | 25-45cm | Shy insects, dangerous subjects |
| Super macro | Variable | Extreme magnification |

## Extension Tubes
- Place between body and lens
- Decrease minimum focus distance
- No optical elements = no quality loss
- Formula: Added mag = extension / focal length
- 36mm tube on 50mm lens = +0.72x magnification

## Close-Up Diopters
- Screw-on magnifying filter
- No light loss
- Maintains infinity focus
- Best quality: achromatic (2-element) like Canon 500D, Raynox DCR-250

## Reversing Rings
- Mount lens backwards on camera
- 50mm reversed = approximately 1:1
- 28mm reversed = approximately 2:1
- Lose all electronic connections
