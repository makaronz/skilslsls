# Macro Subject Techniques

Specialized approaches for common macro photography subjects.

---

## Insects

### Approach Strategy
| Time | Activity | Ease |
|------|----------|------|
| Early morning (cool) | Sluggish, dew-covered | Easiest |
| Midday | Very active | Difficult |
| Overcast | Moderate | Good |

### Approach Tips
- Move slowly, avoid sudden movements
- Approach from front or side
- Pre-focus to approximate distance, lean in
- Don't cast shadow on subject
- Take shots as you approach (start distant)

### Settings
| Setting | Handheld | Tripod |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Aperture | f/5.6-f/8 | f/8-f/11 |
| Shutter | 1/200s+ or flash | Any with flash |
| ISO | 400-1600 | 100-400 |
| Flash | Twin flash recommended | Optional |

## Flowers

| Technique | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| Backlight | Glow through petals |
| Side light | Texture, dimension |
| Dark background | Dramatic isolation |
| Water drops | Added drama (spray bottle) |

## Water Drops
- Medical dropper or electronic controller
- Flash at 1/4-1/16 power to freeze
- Focus on expected splash point
- f/8-f/11, ISO 200
- Colored backgrounds reflect in drops

## Textures/Abstract
- Side lighting at 15-30 degrees for texture
- f/5.6-f/8 for sharpest detail
- Focus stack for complete coverage
- Clean subjects thoroughly (dust is magnified)
- Rotate to find most interesting angle
