# Data Storytelling Examples and Frameworks

Real-world examples and templates for crafting compelling data narratives.

---

## Example 1: Revenue Decline Analysis

### Narrative Arc

**Opening Hook:**
"Last quarter, our revenue declined for the first time in 8 quarters. But the story isn't about decline — it's about a shift we need to understand."

**Setup (Situation):**
- Total revenue: $12.4M (down 7% QoQ)
- Show trend line: 8 quarters of growth, then the dip
- Context: Market grew 3% in same period

**Tension (Complication):**
- Break down by segment: Enterprise grew 12%, SMB declined 22%
- Drill into SMB: New customer acquisition flat, expansion revenue collapsed
- Root cause: Product-market fit shift — SMB needs changed, product didn't adapt

**Resolution:**
- Three recommendations with projected impact
- Recommendation 1: SMB product tier redesign (est. +$800K/quarter)
- Recommendation 2: Win-back campaign for churned SMB (est. +$200K/quarter)
- Recommendation 3: Pricing realignment for SMB segment (est. +$150K/quarter)

**Closing:** "The decline is a signal, not a verdict. These three actions can recover $1.15M in quarterly revenue within two quarters."

## Example 2: Customer Health Dashboard Story

### The Layered Narrative

**Layer 1 — The Headline:**
"67% of our revenue is in healthy accounts — but 18% is at risk of churning this quarter."

**Layer 2 — The Breakdown:**
| Health Status | Accounts | Revenue | % of Total |
|---------------|----------|---------|-----------|
| Healthy (80-100) | 342 | $8.4M | 67% |
| Needs Attention (60-79) | 128 | $1.9M | 15% |
| At Risk (40-59) | 67 | $1.5M | 12% |
| Critical (<40) | 23 | $0.7M | 6% |

**Layer 3 — The Driver:**
"At-risk accounts share three common traits: no executive sponsor engagement in 90+ days, declining feature adoption, and open support escalations."

**Layer 4 — The Action:**
"By activating our intervention playbook for the 23 critical accounts this week, we can protect $700K in ARR. Here's the priority list..."

## Example 3: A/B Test Results Presentation

### Structure

**The Question:** "Does a simplified checkout flow improve conversion?"

**The Experiment:**
- Control: Current 4-step checkout
- Variant: New 2-step checkout
- Duration: 21 days
- Sample: 48,000 visitors (24K per group)

**The Results:**
| Metric | Control | Variant | Change | Significance |
|--------|---------|---------|--------|-------------|
| Conversion rate | 3.2% | 4.1% | +28% | p < 0.001 |
| AOV | $67 | $64 | -4.5% | p = 0.12 (not significant) |
| Revenue per visitor | $2.14 | $2.62 | +22% | p < 0.01 |

**The Insight:** "The simplified checkout significantly increases conversions. While AOV dropped slightly (not statistically significant), revenue per visitor increased 22%."

**The Recommendation:** "Roll out the 2-step checkout to 100% of traffic. Expected annual revenue impact: +$580K."

## Storytelling Templates

### The Comparison Template
"While [benchmark/competitor] achieves [metric], we currently [our metric]. By [action], we can [target] within [timeframe]."

### The Trend Template
"Over the past [period], [metric] has [direction] by [amount]. This is driven by [factors]. If this continues, [projection]. We recommend [actions]."

### The Anomaly Template
"In [period], [metric] showed an unusual [spike/dip] of [amount]. Investigation reveals [root cause]. [Impact assessment]. [Recommended response]."

### The Segmentation Template
"When we look at [overall metric], performance appears [level]. But segmenting by [dimension] reveals [hidden pattern]: [segment A does X] while [segment B does Y]. This suggests [insight]."

## Data Storytelling Checklist

- [ ] Clear narrative arc (setup → tension → resolution)
- [ ] Leading with the key insight (not methodology)
- [ ] Context for every number (vs. what?)
- [ ] Visualizations that support (not replace) the narrative
- [ ] Specific, actionable recommendations
- [ ] Anticipated audience questions addressed
- [ ] Source and date for all data cited
- [ ] Appropriate level of detail for the audience
