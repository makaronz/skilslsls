# Projectbrand Caption Style Guide

Brand identity, voice and tone guidelines, and brand consistency practices for video captioning, subtitling, accessibility, and localization.

---

## Brand Fundamentals

A strong, consistent brand identity in video captioning, subtitling, accessibility, and localization builds recognition, trust, and emotional connection with the audience.

### Brand Identity Components

| Component | Definition | Application |
|---|---|---|
| Brand Voice | Personality expressed through words | All written and spoken content |
| Brand Tone | Emotional variation of voice by context | Adapted per situation |
| Visual Identity | Colors, typography, imagery style | All visual deliverables |
| Brand Values | Core principles and beliefs | Decision-making, messaging |
| Brand Promise | What the audience can expect | Every interaction |

---

## Developing Brand Voice

### Voice Attributes

Define 3-5 core voice attributes that characterize the brand:

- **Dimension 1** — Where does the brand fall on formal vs. casual?
- **Dimension 2** — Technical vs. accessible language?
- **Dimension 3** — Authoritative vs. collaborative?
- **Dimension 4** — Reserved vs. enthusiastic?
- **Dimension 5** — Traditional vs. innovative?

### Voice Application Guide

| Attribute | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Professional | Use clear, precise language | Use slang or colloquialisms |
| Approachable | Write conversationally | Use stiff, bureaucratic language |
| Knowledgeable | Share insights and expertise | Talk down to the audience |
| Confident | Make clear statements | Use hedging language excessively |

---

## Tone Adaptation

While voice remains consistent, tone adapts to context:

| Context | Tone Adjustment | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Educational content | Encouraging, patient | "Here's how to get started..." |
| Error messages | Helpful, reassuring | "Let's fix this together..." |
| Celebrations | Enthusiastic, warm | "Great work! You've achieved..." |
| Formal communications | Respectful, precise | "We appreciate your consideration..." |
| Crisis communication | Calm, direct, transparent | "Here's what we know and what we're doing..." |

---

## Brand Consistency

### Maintaining Consistency Across Channels

- Create and maintain a comprehensive brand style guide
- Train all contributors on brand guidelines
- Conduct regular brand audits across all content
- Provide templates that embed brand standards
- Establish approval processes for brand-adjacent content

### Brand Governance

- Designate brand stewards responsible for consistency
- Create approval workflows for new content types
- Schedule periodic brand reviews to assess consistency
- Update brand guidelines as the brand evolves
- Maintain a centralized library of approved brand assets
