# Video Ad Metrics And Kpis

Measurement frameworks, key performance indicators, and analytics approaches for video advertising, paid video campaigns, and ad creative production.

---

## Measurement Framework

Effective measurement in video advertising, paid video campaigns, and ad creative production requires tracking the right metrics at the right frequency to drive informed decisions and continuous improvement.

### Metric Categories

| Category | Purpose | Update Frequency | Example Metrics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Output Metrics | Measure production volume | Weekly | Items produced, delivery rate |
| Quality Metrics | Measure content quality | Per project | Error rate, review scores, standards compliance |
| Impact Metrics | Measure audience effect | Monthly | Engagement, satisfaction, behavior change |
| Efficiency Metrics | Measure resource use | Monthly | Time per deliverable, cost per unit |
| Growth Metrics | Measure improvement | Quarterly | Skill progression, process optimization |

---

## Key Performance Indicators

### Primary KPIs

These KPIs provide the highest-signal view of performance:

1. **Quality Score** — Composite score based on review feedback, error rates, and standards compliance. Target: consistently above 85%.

2. **Delivery Timeliness** — Percentage of deliverables completed on or before deadline. Target: 90%+ on-time delivery.

3. **Audience Satisfaction** — Measured through feedback, surveys, or engagement metrics. Target: consistent improvement quarter-over-quarter.

4. **Production Efficiency** — Output quality relative to time and resources invested. Target: improving trend without sacrificing quality.

### Supporting Metrics

Additional metrics that provide context and early warning signals:

- Revision cycle count (fewer is better — indicates first-draft quality)
- Stakeholder feedback turnaround time
- Resource utilization rates
- Knowledge reuse rate (templates, components, processes)

## Analytics and Reporting

### Dashboard Design

Build dashboards that surface actionable insights:

- **Executive view** — High-level KPIs, trends, and notable highlights
- **Manager view** — Team performance, workload distribution, quality trends
- **Individual view** — Personal metrics, goals progress, areas for improvement

### Reporting Cadence

| Report | Audience | Frequency | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Status update | Stakeholders | Weekly | Progress, blockers, next steps |
| Quality review | Team leads | Bi-weekly | Quality trends, improvement actions |
| Performance review | Management | Monthly | KPIs, efficiency, capacity planning |
| Strategic review | Leadership | Quarterly | Trends, ROI, strategic alignment |

---

## Using Data for Improvement

Transform measurement into action:

1. **Identify patterns** — Look for recurring issues across projects
2. **Root cause analysis** — Investigate why metrics deviate from targets
3. **Design interventions** — Create specific actions to address identified issues
4. **Measure impact** — Track whether interventions produce desired improvements
5. **Standardize successes** — Incorporate effective changes into standard processes
