# Strategic Planning and Execution

Frameworks for defining product strategy, setting vision, and translating strategy into executable plans.

---

## Product Strategy Components

### Strategy Stack

```
Mission (Why we exist)
    ↓
Vision (Where we're going)
    ↓
Strategy (How we'll get there)
    ↓
Objectives (What we'll achieve this year)
    ↓
Key Results (How we'll measure progress)
    ↓
Initiatives (What we'll build this quarter)
```

### Product Strategy Canvas

| Element | Question | Example |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Vision | What does the world look like if we succeed? | Every team makes data-driven decisions effortlessly |
| Target Customer | Who is our primary user persona? | Mid-market SaaS ops managers |
| Problem | What critical problem do we solve? | Data scattered across 10+ tools |
| Value Proposition | Why do customers choose us? | Single pane of glass with automated insights |
| Competitive Advantage | What is defensible? | Proprietary data connectors + ML models |
| Key Metrics | How do we measure success? | NRR > 120%, DAU/MAU > 40% |
| Revenue Model | How do we make money? | Per-seat subscription + usage-based data |

## Strategic Planning Frameworks

### Opportunity Solution Tree

Visual tree connecting a desired outcome to opportunities and solutions:

```
Desired Outcome: Increase activation rate to 60%
├── Opportunity: Users confused during setup
│   ├── Solution: Interactive setup wizard
│   └── Solution: Video walkthrough
├── Opportunity: Time-to-value too long
│   ├── Solution: Pre-built templates
│   └── Solution: Sample data auto-load
└── Opportunity: Users don't see key features
    ├── Solution: Guided feature tour
    └── Solution: Contextual tooltips
```

### Porter's Five Forces (for Product Context)

Analyze competitive dynamics:

| Force | Questions to Answer | Product Implications |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Competitor Rivalry | How intense is competition? Who is gaining share? | Differentiation priority, positioning |
| Threat of New Entrants | How easy is it to enter our market? | Moat investment (network effects, switching costs) |
| Buyer Power | How easy is it for customers to switch? | Retention features, switching cost creation |
| Supplier Power | How dependent are we on key suppliers (APIs, data sources)? | Diversification, build vs. buy decisions |
| Substitute Threat | What alternatives exist outside our category? | Value proposition clarity, adjacent expansion |

## Execution Frameworks

### North Star Metric

Define a single metric that captures the value your product delivers:

| Company Type | North Star Metric | Why |
|-------------|------------------|-----|
| SaaS | Weekly Active Users completing core action | Measures value delivery |
| Marketplace | Transactions per week | Measures liquidity |
| Media | Time spent consuming content | Measures engagement |
| E-commerce | Revenue per visitor | Measures conversion + AOV |
| Developer Tool | Deployments per week | Measures adoption depth |

### OKR Integration

Translate strategy into quarterly OKRs:

1. **Annual objectives** from the strategy (2-3 themes)
2. **Quarterly objectives** that advance annual themes
3. **Key results** that are measurable and outcome-based
4. **Initiatives** — the projects and features that drive KR movement

### Quarterly Planning Process

| Week | Activity | Participants | Output |
|------|----------|-------------|--------|
| Week -4 | Strategy review and data analysis | Product leadership | Updated strategy brief |
| Week -3 | Opportunity identification | PM, Design, Engineering | Opportunity backlog |
| Week -2 | OKR drafting and initiative scoping | Team leads | Draft OKRs + initiative proposals |
| Week -1 | Cross-functional alignment | All leaders | Finalized OKRs, resource allocation |
| Week 0 | Kickoff and communication | All teams | Shared understanding, work begins |

## Strategy Communication

### One-Page Strategy Document

| Section | Content | Length |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Context | Market trends, competitive landscape, customer insights | 2-3 sentences |
| Strategy Statement | Who we serve, what problem we solve, how we win | 1 sentence |
| Objectives | 3-5 annual objectives | Bullet list |
| Current Quarter Focus | Top 2-3 quarterly OKRs | Brief with metrics |
| Key Bets | Biggest investments and their hypotheses | 2-3 items |
| What We're NOT Doing | Explicit exclusions | 2-3 items |

Keep the strategy document to one page. If it requires more, the strategy is not focused enough.
