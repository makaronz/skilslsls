### Expense Categories

Organize expenses into standard categories with sub-line items:

**Personnel Costs** (typically 60-80% of operating budget):
- Salaries and wages (by department)
- Benefits (health, dental, vision, retirement match)
- Payroll taxes (employer portion)
- Contractor and freelance costs
- Recruiting and onboarding
- Training and development

**Technology & Infrastructure**:
- SaaS subscriptions (itemize tools over $500/month)
- Cloud infrastructure (AWS, Azure, GCP)
- Hardware and equipment
- IT support and maintenance
- Security tools and compliance

**Sales & Marketing**:
- Advertising spend (by channel)
- Content production
- Events and sponsorships
- Sales tools and commissions
- PR and communications

**General & Administrative**:
- Office lease and utilities
- Insurance
- Legal and accounting
- Travel and entertainment
- Office supplies

## Zero-Based Budget Template

Zero-based budgeting (ZBB) requires justifying every expense from zero each period rather than adjusting the prior year's budget.

### Decision Package Format

For each budget item, document:
1. **Activity description**: What does this expense fund?
2. **Purpose and benefit**: Why is it necessary? What happens without it?
3. **Alternatives considered**: What other options exist at lower cost?
4. **Minimum funding level**: What is the bare minimum to operate?
5. **Incremental packages**: What additional funding levels add what value?
6. **Metrics**: How will you measure the return on this investment?

### Priority Ranking

Rank all decision packages by impact and urgency. Fund from the top down until the budget ceiling is reached. This forces explicit tradeoff decisions and eliminates legacy spending that no longer delivers value.
