## Audio Ad Production and Strategy

### Audio Ad Formats

| Format | Duration | Skippable | Best For |
|--------|----------|-----------|----------|
| Audio ad (standard) | 15-30 seconds | No | Brand awareness |
| Podcast ad (host-read) | 30-60 seconds | Sometimes | Authenticity, trust |
| Podcast ad (pre-produced) | 15-30 seconds | Sometimes | Consistency, scale |
| Video takeover | 15-30 seconds | After 5s | Visual impact |
| Sponsored playlist | Ongoing | N/A | Brand association |

### Audio Creative Best Practices

- **Hook immediately**: First 3 seconds must capture attention
- **Conversational tone**: Sound natural, not scripted
- **One clear message**: Focus on a single benefit or CTA
- **Sound design**: Use music and effects to enhance (not distract)
- **Companion display**: Include clickable visual companion when supported
- **Frequency capping**: Limit exposure to prevent listener fatigue

### Production Workflow

1. **Script writing**: Write conversational copy with clear CTA
2. **Voice talent**: Select voice that matches brand personality
3. **Recording**: Professional studio or remote recording setup
4. **Mixing**: Balance voice, music, and sound effects
5. **QA**: Test across headphones, speakers, and car systems
6. **Formats**: Export in required codec and bitrate

### Targeting for Audio

- **Genre targeting**: Reach listeners by music/podcast genre preference
- **Moment targeting**: Time-of-day and activity-based targeting
- **Playlist targeting**: Align with curated playlist themes
- **Demographic targeting**: Age, gender, location
- **Device targeting**: Mobile, desktop, smart speakers, connected cars

## Common Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

| Pitfall | Why It Happens | How to Avoid |
|---------|---------------|---------------|
| Analysis paralysis | Too much data, not enough action | Set decision deadlines, use frameworks |
| Premature scaling | Scaling before validating | Prove ROI at small scale first |
| Ignoring data | Relying on gut feelings | Build data review into process |
| Tool overload | Adding tools without strategy | Audit tool stack quarterly |
| Siloed execution | Teams working independently | Regular cross-functional syncs |
| Inconsistent measurement | Different teams, different metrics | Standardize KPI definitions |
| Set-and-forget | Launching without ongoing optimization | Schedule regular optimization reviews |
