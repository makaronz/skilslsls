# Product Lighting Techniques

Master lighting setups for professional product photography across different product types.

---

## Core Lighting Setups

### White Background (E-commerce Standard)

**Two-Light Setup:**
| Light | Position | Power | Modifier |
|-------|----------|-------|----------|
| Key light | 45° camera-left, above | 1/2-1/1 | Large softbox (36"+) |
| Fill/background | Behind product, aimed at backdrop | 1/2-1 stop above key | Strip softbox or bare |

**Seamless White Technique:**
1. Light background 1-2 stops brighter than product
2. Use V-flat or reflector for fill on shadow side
3. Place product on white acrylic or paper sweep
4. Expose for product, background blows to pure white
5. Goal: RGB 255,255,255 on background

### Dramatic Product (Dark/Moody)

| Light | Purpose | Modifier |
|-------|---------|----------|
| Key light | Define shape, create drama | Strip softbox, gridded |
| Rim/edge light | Separate from background | Strip softbox from behind |
| Accent light | Highlight details | Snoot or focused spot |

### Reflective Products (Glass, Metal, Jewelry)

**Key Principles:**
- Reflective surfaces show the light source shape, not the light color
- Use large, diffused sources for soft, even reflections
- Use black cards to create defined edges and contrast
- Control background reflection carefully

| Product Type | Technique | Key Challenge |
|-------------|-----------|---------------|
| Glassware | Backlight + dark field or bright field | Show transparency and shape |
| Jewelry | Small, precise light sources | Show sparkle without hot spots |
| Chrome/metal | Large softbox reflections | Even, controlled reflection shape |
| Watches | Combination of soft + hard light | Dial legibility + case highlights |

## Product Type Guides

### Electronics
- Matte-finish: Standard softbox setup, avoid specular
- Glossy screens: Angle to show screen content, not reflection
- LED indicators: Dim ambient, long exposure for glow
- Show ports and details with accent light

### Food Photography
- Side/backlight for texture and steam
- Bounce card from front for shadow fill
- Fresnel or hard light for dramatic food styling
- Hero light on main dish, lower power on supporting elements

### Clothing (Flat Lay)
- Even, overhead lighting from two softboxes
- Minimal shadows for true color representation
- Iron/steam all wrinkles before shooting
- Use clips and pins on back side for shape

### Cosmetics/Beauty Products
- Large softbox for creamy highlights on bottles
- Color-accurate lighting (CRI 95+)
- Show texture: matte vs glossy distinction
- Backlighting for transparent liquids
- Multiple angles: straight-on, 3/4, detail shots

## Light Modifier Guide

| Modifier | Light Quality | Best For | Size Recommendation |
|----------|-------------|---------|-------------------|
| Softbox | Soft, even | General product | 24"×36" or larger |
| Strip softbox | Controlled, directional | Edge lighting, reflectives | 12"×48" |
| Beauty dish | Punchy, defined | Round products | 22" |
| Umbrella (shoot-through) | Very soft, wide | Budget setups | 43" |
| Snoot | Hard, focused spot | Accent highlights | Any |
| Ring light | Even, minimal shadow | Small products, flat lay | 18"+ |
| Scrim/diffusion panel | Ultra-soft | Large products | 4×4 ft or larger |

## Common Problems and Fixes

| Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------|---------|
| Uneven white background | Insufficient background lighting | Add background light, increase power |
| Hot spots on reflective products | Light source too small/direct | Larger modifier, increase distance |
| Color cast | Mixed light sources, wrong WB | Match all lights, gray card |
| Flat, lifeless images | Too much fill, no shadow | Reduce fill, add directional key |
| Harsh shadows | Light source too small/close | Larger softbox, add fill card |
