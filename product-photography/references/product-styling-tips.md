# Product Styling Tips

Style and arrange products for maximum visual impact and conversion.

---

## Styling Fundamentals

### The 3-Second Rule
Customers spend ~3 seconds on a product image. The styling must communicate:
1. What the product is
2. Its quality/value
3. Why they need it

### Preparation Checklist

| Step | Details |
|------|---------|
| Clean product thoroughly | Remove fingerprints, dust, lint |
| Remove tags and stickers | Unless they're a feature |
| Steam/iron fabric | Eliminate wrinkles completely |
| Polish reflective surfaces | Microfiber cloth |
| Check for defects | Scratches, dents, imperfections |
| Prepare multiples | Have backup units available |
| Gather styling tools | Clips, tape, putty, pins, stands |

## Styling by Product Type

### Clothing and Apparel

| Method | Pros | Cons | Best For |
|--------|------|------|----------|
| Flat lay | Consistent, scalable | No dimension | T-shirts, simple items |
| Ghost mannequin | Shows fit/shape | Requires compositing | Structured garments |
| On model | Most engaging | Expensive, variable | High-value items |
| Hanger | Quick, simple | Less premium look | Budget catalogs |

**Flat Lay Tips:**
- Use clips on back to create fitted shape
- Fold sleeves consistently across all items
- Stuff collars for structure
- Use tissue paper for volume
- Maintain consistent spacing from frame edges

**Ghost Mannequin Composite:**
1. Shoot on mannequin (front)
2. Remove garment, shoot inside/back on flat
3. Combine in Photoshop: remove mannequin, show interior

### Beverages and Bottles

| Element | Technique |
|---------|-----------|
| Condensation | Spray with glycerin + water mixture (lasts longer) |
| Pour shots | High shutter speed, continuous shooting |
| Label facing | Rotate bottle to show label straight-on |
| Backlight liquid | Show color and transparency |
| Surface droplets | Atomizer spray for fresh look |
| Ice | Use acrylic fake ice for consistency |

### Electronics

| Challenge | Solution |
|-----------|---------|
| Screen glare | Angle screen, add content in post |
| Fingerprints | Handle with gloves, clean before every shot |
| Cable management | Hide cables with tape, routing clips |
| LED indicators | Long exposure in dim environment |
| Showing UI | Add screenshots in post-production |

### Food

| Principle | Implementation |
|-----------|---------------|
| Fresh appearance | Spray with water/oil, use fresh ingredients |
| Hero plate | Build one perfect plate, don't mass produce |
| Undercook slightly | Food photographs best slightly undercooked |
| Color contrast | Use complementary colored props |
| Height and layers | Build upward for dynamic composition |
| Garnish | Fresh herbs, sauces, crumbs for life |

## Props and Backgrounds

### Prop Selection Rules
- Props should support, not compete with, the product
- Stay within the brand's aesthetic
- Use odd numbers of props (1, 3, 5)
- Scale props appropriately to product size
- Keep props in same color family or complementary

### Background Surfaces

| Surface | Feel | Best For |
|---------|------|----------|
| Marble/stone | Luxury, premium | Cosmetics, jewelry, watches |
| Wood (dark) | Warm, rustic | Food, artisanal goods |
| Wood (light) | Clean, Scandinavian | Minimalist brands |
| Concrete/cement | Industrial, modern | Tech, streetwear |
| Fabric/linen | Soft, organic | Skincare, wellness |
| Acrylic (white) | Clean, reflective | Electronics, general |
| Colored paper | Bold, brand-specific | Brand shoots |

## Composition Techniques

### Rule of Thirds
- Place product on intersection points
- Leave breathing room around product
- Direct eye to key features

### Negative Space
- Leave intentional empty space
- Creates premium, aspirational feel
- Useful for adding text overlay later
- 40-60% negative space for lifestyle images

### Grouping
- Hero product front and center, larger
- Supporting products behind or to the side
- Create depth with overlapping placement
- Use odd numbers for natural grouping
