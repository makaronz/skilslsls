# Location Sound

Audio production techniques, sound design principles, and recording best practices for documentary filmmaking, non-fiction storytelling, and real-world film production.

---

## Audio Fundamentals

High-quality audio is essential in documentary filmmaking, non-fiction storytelling, and real-world film production, often making the difference between professional and amateur results. Audiences are typically more forgiving of visual imperfections than poor audio quality.

### Key Audio Parameters

| Parameter | Description | Professional Standard |
|---|---|---|
| Sample Rate | Number of samples per second | 48 kHz (video), 44.1 kHz (audio-only) |
| Bit Depth | Dynamic range resolution | 24-bit recording, 16-bit delivery |
| Loudness | Perceived audio level | -16 LUFS (streaming), -24 LUFS (broadcast) |
| Noise Floor | Background noise level | Below -60 dB |
| Dynamic Range | Difference between quietest and loudest | Controlled with compression |

## Recording Best Practices

### Environment Preparation

- **Minimize ambient noise** — Turn off HVAC, phones, appliances; close windows
- **Treat the room** — Use acoustic panels, blankets, or other absorption to reduce reflections
- **Monitor continuously** — Use headphones to catch problems in real-time
- **Record room tone** — Capture 30-60 seconds of silence for editing use

### Microphone Technique

- Select the appropriate microphone type for the source (dynamic, condenser, lavalier)
- Position the microphone at the optimal distance and angle for the source
- Use pop filters for vocal recording to reduce plosive sounds
- Test and adjust levels before the actual recording session

## Post-Production Audio

### Editing Workflow

1. **Organize clips** — Label and sort all audio files systematically
2. **Clean up** — Remove noise, clicks, pops, and unwanted sounds
3. **Edit for content** — Arrange, trim, and sequence audio elements
4. **Mix** — Balance levels, apply EQ, compression, and spatial effects
5. **Master** — Final processing for consistent loudness and tonal quality

### Essential Processing

- **Equalization (EQ)** — Shape the frequency content to enhance clarity and presence
- **Compression** — Control dynamic range for consistent listening levels
- **Noise reduction** — Remove persistent background noise without degrading quality
- **De-essing** — Reduce harsh sibilant sounds in vocal recordings
- **Reverb/Space** — Add appropriate spatial character when needed

## Audio Quality Checklist

- [ ] Recording levels peak between -12 dB and -6 dB
- [ ] No clipping or distortion present
- [ ] Background noise below acceptable threshold
- [ ] Consistent audio levels throughout
- [ ] Clear, intelligible speech (if applicable)
- [ ] Appropriate loudness for the delivery platform
- [ ] All transitions smooth and natural
