# Initial Research Phase

Search engine optimization strategies and search visibility techniques for documentary filmmaking, non-fiction storytelling, and real-world film production.

---

## SEO Fundamentals

Search optimization in documentary filmmaking, non-fiction storytelling, and real-world film production ensures content is discoverable by the target audience through organic search channels.

### Core SEO Elements

| Element | Purpose | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Keyword Research | Identify what the audience searches for | Foundation of SEO strategy |
| On-Page Optimization | Optimize content elements for search | Direct ranking factor |
| Technical SEO | Ensure search engines can access and index content | Prerequisite for visibility |
| Content Quality | Provide genuine value to searchers | Primary ranking signal |
| Authority Building | Establish credibility and trust | Long-term ranking power |

---

## Keyword Strategy

### Research Process

1. **Brainstorm seed keywords** — Start with core topics relevant to the content
2. **Expand with tools** — Use keyword research tools to discover related terms and questions
3. **Analyze intent** — Categorize keywords by user intent (informational, navigational, transactional)
4. **Assess competition** — Evaluate difficulty and opportunity for each keyword
5. **Prioritize** — Select keywords that balance search volume, relevance, and achievable ranking

### Keyword Integration

- **Title/headline** — Include primary keyword naturally
- **Headings** — Use related keywords in subheadings
- **Body content** — Integrate keywords naturally without forced repetition
- **Meta description** — Include primary keyword in the page description
- **Alt text** — Describe visual content with relevant keywords

---

## On-Page Optimization

### Content Optimization Checklist

- [ ] Primary keyword in the title/H1 heading
- [ ] Descriptive, keyword-rich meta description
- [ ] Logical heading hierarchy (H1 > H2 > H3)
- [ ] Internal links to related content
- [ ] External links to authoritative sources
- [ ] Images optimized with descriptive alt text
- [ ] Content comprehensively covers the topic
- [ ] Readable, scannable formatting

---

## Content Quality Signals

Search engines increasingly prioritize content that demonstrates:

- **Experience** — First-hand knowledge of the topic
- **Expertise** — Demonstrated skill and knowledge
- **Authoritativeness** — Recognition as a trusted source
- **Trustworthiness** — Accurate, transparent, and reliable content

## Measuring SEO Performance

| Metric | What It Measures | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Organic traffic | Visitors from search engines | Steady growth |
| Keyword rankings | Position in search results | Top 10 for primary terms |
| Click-through rate | Percentage of impressions that result in clicks | Above average for position |
| Bounce rate | Visitors who leave without engaging | Decreasing trend |
| Conversion rate | Visitors who take desired action | Meeting business goals |
