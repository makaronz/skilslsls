# Self Distribution

Distribution, delivery, and publication strategies for documentary filmmaking, non-fiction storytelling, and real-world film production to ensure content reaches the intended audience effectively.

---

## Distribution Strategy

Effective distribution ensures that high-quality work in documentary filmmaking, non-fiction storytelling, and real-world film production reaches the right audience through the right channels at the right time.

### Channel Selection

| Channel Type | Reach | Control | Cost | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Owned Channels | Medium | High | Low | Core audience, brand building |
| Platform Distribution | High | Medium | Variable | Broad reach, discovery |
| Partner Channels | Medium | Low | Variable | Targeted audiences, credibility |
| Paid Distribution | High | High | High | Rapid reach, specific targeting |

---

## Delivery Specifications

### Format Requirements

Prepare deliverables in formats optimized for each distribution channel:

- **Digital platforms** — Follow platform-specific format, size, and resolution requirements
- **Print/physical** — Ensure resolution, color space, and bleed specifications are met
- **Mobile** — Optimize for mobile viewing (responsive, fast-loading, touch-friendly)
- **Accessibility** — Include alternative formats (captions, transcripts, alt text) as needed

### Quality Assurance for Distribution

Before publishing or distributing:

1. Verify final content against approved specifications
2. Test on representative devices and platforms
3. Confirm all links, references, and interactive elements function correctly
4. Validate that metadata is complete and accurate
5. Ensure compliance with platform guidelines and policies

---

## Timing and Scheduling

### Publication Timing

- Research optimal publication times for target audience and platform
- Consider time zones if the audience is geographically distributed
- Avoid competing with major events or seasonal conflicts
- Build a consistent publishing cadence when applicable

### Launch Planning

For significant releases:

- **Pre-launch** — Build awareness and anticipation through teasers and announcements
- **Launch** — Coordinate simultaneous distribution across selected channels
- **Post-launch** — Monitor performance, engage with audience, and amplify successful elements

---

## Performance Monitoring

Track distribution effectiveness:

- **Reach** — How many people were exposed to the content?
- **Engagement** — How did the audience interact with it?
- **Conversion** — Did the content drive desired actions?
- **Feedback** — What qualitative responses were received?

Use these insights to refine future distribution strategies and channel selection for subsequent projects.

---

## Archive and Maintenance

- Maintain organized archives of all distributed content
- Schedule periodic reviews of published content for continued accuracy
- Plan for content updates and refreshes as needed
- Document distribution history for reporting and planning purposes
