# Fashion Studio Lighting Setups

Professional lighting configurations for editorial and commercial fashion photography.

---

## Classic Fashion Setups

### Beauty/Clamshell Lighting
| Light | Position | Modifier | Power |
|-------|----------|----------|-------|
| Key | Directly above, angled down 45° | Large octabox (48-60") | Full |
| Fill | Below face, angled up | Silver reflector or 2nd light | -2 stops |
| Background | Behind subject | 2 strip boxes or gelled spots | Variable |

**Effect:** Minimal shadows, glamorous, even illumination. Ideal for beauty campaigns and cosmetics.

### High-Fashion Editorial
| Light | Position | Modifier | Power |
|-------|----------|----------|-------|
| Key | High, 60-90° to side | Silver beauty dish or bare head | Full |
| Accent | Opposite side, behind | Strip box with grid | -1 stop |
| Background | Separate, creative gels | Spot or strip | Variable |

**Effect:** Strong contrast, dramatic shadows, editorial impact.

### Catalogue/E-commerce
| Light | Position | Modifier | Power |
|-------|----------|----------|-------|
| Key | 45° camera-left, slightly above | Large softbox (48"×72") | Full |
| Fill | Camera-right, or large reflector | Softbox or V-flat | -1.5 stops |
| Background | Behind, even coverage | 2 lights or strip boxes | +1 stop above key |

**Effect:** Even, clean, accurate color representation. White or neutral background.

## Advanced Techniques

### Cross Lighting (Dual Key)
- Two strip boxes at 90° on either side
- Creates dramatic edge lighting
- Works for silhouette-style editorial
- Subject between lights, camera centered

### Rim Light with Negative Fill
- Key from behind/side for rim
- V-flats (black side) on front to absorb fill
- Creates high-contrast, moody fashion look
- Subject outline is bright, front is mostly shadow

### Movement and Motion Setups
| Technique | Equipment | Settings |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Frozen motion | High-power strobe (1/4000s+ t.1) | f/8-f/11, 100 ISO |
| Motion blur + sharp | Rear curtain sync | 1/15-1/60, f/8 |
| Continuous for flow | LED panels (high output) | 1/250-1/500, wide aperture |

## Color Gel Usage

| Gel Color | Effect | Common Use |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| CTO (orange) | Warm accent | Sunset feel, warm tones |
| CTB (blue) | Cool accent | Night feel, contrast with skin |
| Magenta | Bold, fashion-forward | Editorial, beauty |
| Red | Dramatic, intense | High-fashion editorial |
| Green | Edgy, avant-garde | Creative editorial |
| Purple | Luxurious, mystical | Fantasy, beauty |

**Gel Placement Tips:**
- Gels on background or accent lights (not key on skin)
- Use complementary gel pairs for contrast
- Lower power when using gels (colors saturate at lower power)
- Double-gel for deeper color saturation

## Equipment for Fashion

### Recommended Strobes
| Use | Type | Power | Features Needed |
|-----|------|-------|----------------|
| Beauty | Monolight | 300-500Ws | Fast recycle, modeling light |
| Editorial | Pack + head | 1000-2400Ws | High power, fast flash duration |
| On-location | Battery strobe | 400-600Ws | Portable, TTL capability |

### Must-Have Modifiers
- 48" octabox (beauty key light)
- 12"×48" strip box with grid (rim/accent)
- 22" silver beauty dish (punchy fashion light)
- 7" standard reflector with grids (spot accent)
- 4×4 ft scrim frame (large diffusion)
- V-flats: 2 white, 2 black (fill and negative fill)
