# Fashion Posing and Model Direction

Direct models for dynamic, editorial, and commercial fashion imagery.

---

## Posing Foundations

### Body Angles

| Angle | Effect | Best For |
|-------|--------|----------|
| Straight-on | Powerful, confrontational | Editorial, streetwear |
| 3/4 turn | Slimming, classic | Most fashion, catalog |
| Profile | Dramatic, editorial | Accessories, profile features |
| Back turn with head | Mystery, reveals back details | Gowns, back designs |
| 45° from camera | Dynamic, dimensional | Versatile default |

### Posture Essentials
- Elongate the spine ("imagine a string pulling from top of head")
- Shoulders back and down (never hunched)
- Weight on one foot (creates natural S-curve)
- Create space between arms and torso (slimming)
- Engage core (stands taller, looks more confident)

## Movement Direction

### Walking Shots

| Style | Direction | Mood |
|-------|-----------|------|
| Confident stride | Walk toward camera | Power, editorial |
| Casual walk | Walk past camera at angle | Lifestyle, street |
| Runway walk | Crossover steps, purpose | High fashion |
| Looking back | Walk away, turn head | Mystery, intrigue |

### Dynamic Movement
- Spinning for fabric flow (gowns, skirts)
- Hair toss with body movement
- Jumping (freeze with strobe)
- Fabric throw or catch
- Wind machine for controlled flow

### Communication Style
- Give action-based directions: "Walk toward me and stop"
- Avoid: "Look sexy" or vague emotional cues
- Use: "Think about someone you love seeing you for the first time"
- Demo the pose yourself — even imperfectly
- Play music matching the desired mood
- Keep the energy high with positive feedback

## Pose Types by Garment

### Dresses and Gowns
- Full-length shots with fabric spread
- Movement shots for flow
- One hand gathering fabric at side
- Seated with fabric arranged
- Walking with train trailing

### Pants and Bottoms
- Walking shots for fit and drape
- One hand in pocket, hip shifted
- Seated cross-legged (shows cut)
- From-behind shot for back pocket detail

### Jackets and Outerwear
- Collar popped or adjusted
- One shoulder dropped off
- Hand in pocket, jacket open
- Buttoning/unbuttoning action
- Wind-blown open jacket

### Jewelry and Accessories
- Hand near face (earrings, necklaces)
- Arm extended (bracelets, watches)
- Close-up with deliberate hand pose
- Hair pulled back for earring visibility

## Editorial vs Commercial Posing

| Aspect | Editorial | Commercial |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| Expression | Serious, intense, mysterious | Approachable, smiling, relatable |
| Poses | Extreme angles, unusual shapes | Natural, aspirational |
| Hands | Expressive, dramatic | Relaxed, purposeful |
| Eye contact | Often indirect, distant | Often direct, engaging |
| Energy | Moody, powerful | Energetic, positive |
| Clothing focus | Concept over garment detail | Garment clarity prioritized |

## Model Preparation

### Pre-Shoot Checklist
- Share mood board in advance
- Discuss wardrobe and styling plan
- Review model's portfolio for natural strengths
- Prepare pose reference images
- Confirm hair and makeup direction
- Discuss any physical limitations or preferences

### On-Set Flow
1. **Warm up**: Simple standing poses to build comfort (5 min)
2. **Static poses**: Key looks, methodical coverage (20-30 min)
3. **Movement**: Dynamic poses, walking, interaction (15-20 min)
4. **Details**: Close-ups, accessories, texture shots (10 min)
5. **Creative**: Experimental, editorial, collaborative (10-15 min)
