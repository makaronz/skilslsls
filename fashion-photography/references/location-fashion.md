# Location Fashion Photography

Produce fashion imagery on location with environmental context and natural light.

---

## Location Selection

### Location Types

| Location | Aesthetic | Challenges | Best For |
|----------|----------|-----------|----------|
| Urban/city | Edgy, modern | Permits, crowds, traffic | Streetwear, modern fashion |
| Beach/coastal | Fresh, aspirational | Wind, sand, reflections | Swimwear, resort wear |
| Desert/arid | Dramatic, minimal | Heat, limited shade | Editorial, boho fashion |
| Forest/garden | Natural, romantic | Uneven light, insects | Organic brands, bridal |
| Industrial | Gritty, fashion-forward | Safety, lighting | Editorial, avant-garde |
| Historic architecture | Luxurious, classic | Permits, limitations | Formal wear, luxury |
| Rooftop/skyline | Urban, aspirational | Wind, access | Business, lifestyle |

### Scouting Checklist
- [ ] Visit at same time of day as shoot
- [ ] Note sun direction and shadow patterns
- [ ] Identify power sources for lighting
- [ ] Check permit requirements
- [ ] Assess parking and load-in logistics
- [ ] Scout backup locations in case of weather
- [ ] Identify changing areas for wardrobe
- [ ] Note background distractions to avoid

## Natural Light Strategies

### Time of Day Guide

| Time | Light Quality | Fashion Use |
|------|-------------|------------|
| Blue hour (pre-dawn) | Cool, even, moody | Editorial, dramatic |
| Golden hour (dawn) | Warm, directional, soft | Romantic, luxury |
| Morning (2h after sunrise) | Warm but brighter | Lifestyle, catalog |
| Midday | Harsh, overhead | Avoid or use shade/scrim |
| Afternoon | Warm, directional | Lifestyle, action |
| Golden hour (sunset) | Warm, dramatic | Beauty, editorial |
| Blue hour (post-sunset) | Cool, even, moody | Evening wear, editorial |

### Managing Natural Light

| Challenge | Solution |
|-----------|---------|
| Harsh midday sun | Move to open shade, use large scrim |
| Backlit subject | Use reflector for fill, expose for subject |
| Dappled light (under trees) | Find even shade or use scrim above |
| Overcast flat light | Use reflector for direction, shoot tight |
| Changing clouds | Shoot in manual, adjust frequently |

## Mixing Ambient and Flash

### Fill Flash on Location
- Purpose: Open shadows without overpowering ambient
- Power: -1 to -2 stops below ambient
- Modifier: Large portable softbox or beauty dish
- Sync: High-speed sync (HSS) for wide apertures in daylight

### Overpowering Sun
- Purpose: Dramatic, studio-like look on location
- Power: Match or exceed ambient
- Modifier: Beauty dish or bare head with grid
- Settings: f/8-f/11, ISO 100, max sync speed
- Result: Dark sky, perfectly lit subject

## Logistics

### Essential Location Kit

| Category | Items |
|----------|-------|
| Lighting | 1-2 battery strobes, stands, modifiers, reflector |
| Grip | Sandbags, C-stands, gaffer tape, clamps |
| Camera | Main body + backup, portrait + wide lenses |
| Wardrobe | Steamer, clips, safety pins, lint roller |
| Comfort | Water, snacks, shade tent, seating |
| Safety | First aid kit, sunscreen, emergency contacts |
| Admin | Permits, release forms, call sheet |

### Weather Contingency
- Always have indoor backup location scouted
- Rain: Use covered areas (archways, overhangs) for moody shots
- Wind: Bring clips and weights; use wind for dynamic fabric movement
- Extreme heat: Schedule shorter bursts with shade breaks
- Cold: Layer model between shots, have warm drinks available
