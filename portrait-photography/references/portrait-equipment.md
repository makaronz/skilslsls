# Portrait Photography Equipment Guide

Select the right camera gear, lenses, and lighting equipment for portrait photography.

---

## Lens Selection

### Portrait Lens Comparison

| Focal Length | Perspective | Best For | Background Blur | Working Distance |
|-------------|-----------|---------|-----------------|-----------------|
| 35mm | Wide, environmental | Environmental, full-body | Moderate | Close |
| 50mm f/1.4-1.8 | Natural, slight compression | Versatile, budget-friendly | Good | Medium |
| 85mm f/1.4-1.8 | Classic portrait compression | Head/shoulders, 3/4 | Excellent | Comfortable |
| 105mm f/2.8 | Strong compression | Tight headshots, beauty | Very good | Further |
| 135mm f/2 | Maximum compression | Headshots, blur-heavy | Outstanding | Far |
| 70-200mm f/2.8 | Versatile zoom | Events, varied framing | Excellent | Variable |
| 24-70mm f/2.8 | Wide to normal zoom | Environmental, groups | Good | Variable |

### Lens Characteristics for Portraits

**Ideal Portrait Lens Qualities:**
- Fast aperture (f/1.4-f/2.8) for shallow DOF and low light
- Smooth, pleasant bokeh (out-of-focus quality)
- Minimal distortion at portrait distances
- Sharp at typical portrait apertures (f/2-f/5.6)
- Good autofocus with eye detection support
- Reasonable size and weight for extended shoots

## Lighting Equipment

### Studio Strobes vs Continuous

| Factor | Strobes | Continuous (LED) |
|--------|---------|------------------|
| Power | High | Moderate |
| Color consistency | Excellent | Good (high CRI models) |
| Flash duration | Freezes motion | Ambient dependent |
| Modeling light | Yes (low power) | What you see is what you get |
| Video capability | No | Yes |
| Heat output | Low (flash) | Can be high |
| Client preview | Need test shots | See lighting live |
| Cost (quality) | $300-2000+ | $200-1500+ |

### Recommended Lighting Kits

**Starter Kit ($300-600):**
- 1 speedlight with trigger
- 1 collapsible softbox (24-32")
- 1 light stand
- 1 5-in-1 reflector (32-42")

**Professional Kit ($1,500-4,000):**
- 2-3 studio strobes (300-500Ws)
- 1 large softbox (48"+)
- 1 beauty dish (22")
- 1 strip softbox
- 3 light stands (heavy duty)
- Backdrop system + muslin/paper
- V-flats (2 white, 1 black)
