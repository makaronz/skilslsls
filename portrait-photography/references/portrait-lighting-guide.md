# Portrait Lighting Guide

Create professional portrait lighting with natural and studio light sources.

---

## Classic Lighting Patterns

### Pattern Reference

| Pattern | Key Light Position | Shadow Character | Best For |
|---------|-------------------|-----------------|----------|
| Flat | Directly in front, at eye level | Minimal | Beauty, cosmetics |
| Loop | 30-45° side, slightly above | Small nose shadow pointing down | Most subjects (versatile) |
| Rembrandt | 45° side, above | Triangle under eye on shadow side | Dramatic, character |
| Split | 90° side | Half face lit, half dark | Very dramatic, moody |
| Butterfly | Directly above, in front | Shadow under nose (butterfly shape) | Beauty, fashion |
| Clamshell | Above + below (fill from under) | Minimal, even | Beauty, glamour |

### Light Ratios

| Ratio | Effect | Mood |
|-------|--------|------|
| 1:1 | No shadow difference | Flat, even, beauty |
| 2:1 | Subtle shadow | Natural, flattering |
| 3:1 | Moderate contrast | Character, classic |
| 4:1 | Strong shadow | Dramatic, moody |
| 8:1+ | Deep shadows | Very dramatic, film noir |

**Measuring Ratio:**
- Meter key side, then shadow side
- 1 stop difference = 2:1 ratio
- 2 stops = 4:1 ratio
- 3 stops = 8:1 ratio

## Natural Light Portraits

### Window Light
- Large window = soft, directional light
- Subject 2-4 feet from window
- Sheer curtain for extra diffusion
- White reflector opposite window for fill
- Time of day affects color temperature and direction

### Golden Hour Outdoor
| Approach | Setup | Effect |
|----------|-------|--------|
| Front-lit | Sun behind camera | Warm, even light |
| Side-lit | Sun at 90° | Dimension, texture |
| Back-lit | Sun behind subject | Rim glow, dreamy |
| Open shade + reflector | Shade + gold reflector | Soft + warm fill |

### Overcast Light
- Natural softbox (even, diffused)
- Can be flat — add direction with reflector or flag
- Excellent for skin tones and color accuracy
- No harsh shadows to manage

## Studio Lighting Setups

### One-Light Setup
- Single softbox at 45° = versatile loop/Rembrandt
- Add V-flat or reflector for fill
- Most efficient and often most flattering

### Two-Light Setup
| Configuration | Key Light | Fill/Second Light | Use |
|-------------|-----------|------------------|-----|
| Key + fill | 45° softbox | Front reflector or -2 stop light | Classic portrait |
| Key + rim | 45° softbox | Behind subject, opposite side | Separation from BG |
| Key + background | 45° softbox | Aimed at backdrop | Controlled BG brightness |

### Three+ Light Setup
- Key light: Main illumination
- Fill light: Shadow control
- Rim/hair light: Subject-background separation
- Background light: Control backdrop brightness/color
- Optional: Accent light for specific features

## Modifier Selection for Portraits

| Modifier | Size | Quality | Best For |
|----------|------|---------|----------|
| Large softbox (48"+) | Big | Very soft | Groups, full-body, flattering skin |
| Medium softbox (36") | Medium | Soft with definition | Head/shoulders, standard portraits |
| Octabox | Round | Soft, round catchlights | Beauty, fashion |
| Beauty dish | 22" | Punchy, defined | Beauty, fashion close-ups |
| Umbrella (silver) | Large | Contrasty, specular | Efficient group lighting |
| Strip softbox | Narrow | Directional | Rim light, edge definition |
| Ring light | Even | Flat, even, fashion | Beauty, content creation |

## Common Issues and Solutions

| Issue | Cause | Solution |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Raccoon eyes | Light too high | Lower key light to eye level or slightly above |
| Double shadow | Multiple equal-power lights | Make one dominant (key), reduce other |
| Flat lighting | Light too close to camera axis | Move key light to 30-45° angle |
| Shiny skin | Specular reflection | Use polarizer, blot with tissue, matte makeup |
| Dark eye sockets | Deep-set eyes + overhead light | Add low fill, reflector under face |
