# OKR Examples

Practical OKR examples across functions and industries to guide effective goal-setting.

---

## Company-Level OKRs

### Growth-Stage SaaS Company

**Objective**: Achieve product-market fit in the mid-market segment

| Key Result | Baseline | Target | Measurement |
|-----------|----------|--------|-------------|
| Increase mid-market NRR from 95% to 115% | 95% | 115% | Billing system |
| Grow mid-market ARR from $2M to $5M | $2M | $5M | CRM |
| Achieve mid-market NPS of 50+ | 28 | 50+ | Quarterly survey |

**Objective**: Build a world-class engineering culture

| Key Result | Baseline | Target | Measurement |
|-----------|----------|--------|-------------|
| Reduce mean time to recovery from 4 hours to 30 minutes | 4 hrs | 30 min | Incident tracker |
| Increase deployment frequency from weekly to daily | Weekly | Daily | CI/CD metrics |
| Achieve 90% score on quarterly developer experience survey | 72% | 90% | Internal survey |

## OKR Anti-Patterns to Avoid

| Anti-Pattern | Example | Problem | Fix |
|-------------|---------|---------|-----|
| Activity as KR | "Publish 10 blog posts" | Measures output, not outcome | "Generate 500 MQLs from content" |
| Binary KR | "Launch feature X" | No gradient of success | "Achieve 30% adoption of feature X within 30 days" |
| Sandbagged KR | "Maintain current NPS" | No stretch, no growth | "Improve NPS from 32 to 50" |
| Uncontrollable KR | "Achieve $10M valuation" | Team cannot directly influence | Choose metrics within team's control |
| Vanity KR | "Reach 1M page views" | Does not drive business outcomes | Tie to conversion or engagement metric |
