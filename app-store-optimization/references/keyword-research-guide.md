# Keyword Research Guide

Systematic approach to discovering, evaluating, and deploying keywords for App Store and Google Play optimization.

---

## Keyword Research Process

### Step 1: Seed Keyword Generation

Build an initial keyword list from multiple sources:

- **Core features**: List every feature your app provides and translate each into user search terms
- **User language**: Mine app reviews (yours and competitors) for the exact words users use to describe the problem your app solves
- **Competitor metadata**: Analyze the title, subtitle, and keyword field of top 10 competitors in your category
- **Auto-suggest**: Type seed terms into the App Store and Google Play search bars and record all auto-complete suggestions
- **Search ads data**: If running Apple Search Ads, export the Search Match terms report for real query data

### Step 2: Keyword Evaluation

Score each keyword on three dimensions:

| Dimension | Description | Measurement |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Relevance** | How closely the keyword matches your app's purpose | Manual 1-5 score |
| **Search Volume** | How many users search for this term monthly | ASO tool traffic score |
| **Difficulty** | How hard it is to rank in the top 10 | ASO tool competition score |

Prioritize keywords with high relevance, moderate-to-high volume, and low-to-moderate difficulty. Avoid high-difficulty keywords unless your app already has strong category authority.

### Step 3: Keyword Mapping

Assign keywords to specific metadata fields based on priority:

**iOS Keyword Allocation**:
- **Title** (30 chars): Highest-priority keyword + brand name
- **Subtitle** (30 chars): Second-priority keyword phrase
- **Keyword Field** (100 chars): Remaining keywords, comma-separated, no spaces, no duplicates of title/subtitle words

**Google Play Keyword Allocation**:
- **Title** (30 chars): Primary keyword + brand name
- **Short Description** (80 chars): Key feature keywords in a natural sentence
- **Long Description** (4000 chars): Use target keywords 3-5 times naturally throughout the text

## Advanced Techniques

### Long-Tail Keyword Strategy

Long-tail keywords (3+ words) typically have lower volume but higher conversion rates because they signal specific intent. Examples:
- Instead of "fitness app" → "home workout app no equipment"
- Instead of "budget tracker" → "budget tracker for college students"

Build long-tail variants by combining core terms with modifiers: "free," "best," "for [audience]," "without [limitation]," "offline," "simple."

### Localization Keyword Research

For each target market:
1. Do not directly translate English keywords — research native search behavior
2. Use local ASO tools or native speakers to validate keyword choices
3. Monitor local competitors who may rank for terms you are missing
4. Adapt to local naming conventions (e.g., "torch" vs. "flashlight" in UK vs. US English)

### Seasonal Keyword Optimization

Identify keywords with seasonal traffic spikes and update metadata in advance:
- Tax apps: Update keywords 4-6 weeks before tax season
- Fitness apps: Refresh keywords in late December for New Year resolution traffic
- Shopping apps: Target Black Friday and holiday keywords starting in October

## Keyword Maintenance Cadence

| Frequency | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Weekly | Monitor ranking changes for tracked keywords |
| Bi-weekly | Review Apple Search Ads search term reports for new keyword ideas |
| Monthly | Refresh keyword difficulty scores and adjust targeting |
| Quarterly | Full keyword audit — add new terms, retire underperformers, update metadata |
