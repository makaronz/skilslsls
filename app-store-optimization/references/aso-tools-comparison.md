## Feature Deep Dive

### Sensor Tower

Sensor Tower provides the most comprehensive dataset for enterprise ASO teams. Key capabilities include:
- **App Intelligence**: Download and revenue estimates with country-level granularity
- **Store Intelligence**: Top charts tracking, category rankings, and featured app monitoring
- **Ad Intelligence**: Visibility into competitor paid acquisition strategies across ad networks
- **Keyword Intelligence**: Search volume scores, difficulty ratings, and keyword suggestions based on semantic analysis
- **Usage Intelligence**: DAU/MAU estimates and demographic breakdowns (premium tier)

Best for: Large publishers, agencies managing multiple apps, and competitive intelligence teams. Pricing starts around $500/month for basic plans.

### AppTweak

AppTweak strikes a balance between depth and usability. Notable features:
- **Keyword research**: Search volume scores, difficulty index, and keyword suggestions powered by AI
- **ASO Timeline**: Track metadata changes and correlate with ranking movements
- **A/B Test Monitoring**: Detect when competitors run store listing experiments
- **Review Management**: Sentiment analysis with auto-categorization by topic
- **API Access**: Integrate ASO data into internal dashboards and workflows

Best for: Mid-size studios and ASO specialists who need depth without enterprise pricing.

### AppFollow

AppFollow specializes in review management and reputation monitoring:
- **Review Aggregation**: Centralize reviews from both stores with translation support
- **Auto-Replies**: Configure template responses based on star rating or keyword detection
- **Sentiment Tracking**: Longitudinal sentiment scoring to measure the impact of updates
- **Slack/Jira Integration**: Route critical reviews to support or product teams instantly

Best for: Teams where review management and user feedback loops are top priorities.

## Selection Criteria

When choosing an ASO tool, evaluate against these criteria:

1. **Data accuracy**: Compare keyword volume estimates against your own search ads data
2. **Update frequency**: Ranking and keyword data should refresh daily at minimum
3. **Geographic coverage**: Ensure the tool covers all markets where your app operates
4. **API availability**: Critical for teams that build custom dashboards or automate reporting
5. **Team collaboration**: Multi-seat pricing, role-based access, and shared dashboards
6. **Integration ecosystem**: Connections to Slack, Jira, analytics platforms, and attribution tools

## Recommended Stack by Team Size

- **Solo developer**: AppRadar or TheTool for budget-friendly keyword research
- **Small team (2-5)**: AppTweak for balanced features and pricing
- **Growth team (5-15)**: Sensor Tower or data.ai for comprehensive competitive intelligence
- **Agency**: Sensor Tower with multi-app management and white-label reporting
