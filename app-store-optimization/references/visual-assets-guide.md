# Visual Assets Guide

Best practices for designing app icons, screenshots, and preview videos that maximize conversion rates in the App Store and Google Play.

---

## App Icon Design

The app icon is the single most viewed creative asset. Optimize it for instant recognition at small sizes.

### Design Principles

| Principle | Do | Don't |
|-----------|-----|-------|
| Simplicity | Use one focal element | Cram multiple objects or text |
| Color | Use bold, contrasting colors | Use muted or similar tones |
| Shape | Leverage the platform's mask (rounded square for iOS, adaptive for Android) | Design to the edge without padding |
| Uniqueness | Differentiate from category competitors | Copy the dominant color/style of the top app |
| Scalability | Test at 16×16px, 29×29px, and 1024×1024px | Only review at full resolution |

### Platform Specifications

- **iOS**: 1024×1024px (PNG, no alpha, no rounded corners — the system applies the mask)
- **Google Play**: 512×512px (PNG, 32-bit with alpha)

### A/B Testing Icons

Run icon tests using Google Play Store listing experiments or third-party tools (StoreMaven, SplitMetrics). Test one variable at a time:
1. Background color
2. Focal element style (flat vs. 3D)
3. With vs. without subtle text or badge

## Screenshot Strategy

Screenshots are the primary conversion driver on store listing pages. Design them as a visual sales pitch, not a feature tour.

### First-Screenshot Framework

The first screenshot gets the most views by far. Structure it as:
- **Headline** (top 20%): One benefit-driven statement in large text
- **Device Frame** (center 60%): Show the app's most compelling screen
- **Supporting Text** (bottom 20%): Optional subtext or social proof

### Screenshot Sequence Blueprint

| Position | Purpose | Content Strategy |
|----------|---------|-----------------|
| 1 | Hook | Core value proposition, hero feature |
| 2 | Key Feature | Most-used or differentiating feature |
| 3 | Social Proof | Ratings, awards, user count, press quotes |
| 4 | Secondary Feature | Another compelling capability |
| 5 | Call to Action | Download prompt or special offer |

### Design Specifications

- **iOS**: Up to 10 screenshots per localization. Sizes: 6.7" (1290×2796), 6.5" (1284×2778), 5.5" (1242×2208), iPad 12.9" (2048×2732)
- **Google Play**: Up to 8 screenshots. Min 320px, max 3840px on any dimension, 16:9 or 9:16 aspect ratio

### Localization

Translate screenshot text for each target market. Beyond translation, adapt:
- Currency symbols and number formats
- Cultural references and imagery
- Color associations (red means luck in China, danger in Western markets)
- Example content (use locally relevant names, cities, products)

## App Preview Videos

### iOS App Preview

- Length: 15-30 seconds
- Format: H.264, up to 500 MB
- Resolution: Match device screenshot dimensions
- Auto-plays muted on Wi-Fi in the App Store

### Google Play Promo Video

- Hosted on YouTube (link in store listing)
- Recommended length: 30-120 seconds
- First 5 seconds are critical — show the app immediately, not a logo intro

### Video Best Practices

1. **Show the app in action** within the first 3 seconds
2. **Add text overlays** since videos auto-play muted
3. **Focus on 2-3 features** maximum — don't try to show everything
4. **End with a clear CTA** and your brand name
5. **Use real device footage** rather than abstract motion graphics
